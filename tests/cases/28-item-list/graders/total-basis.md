PASS if the answer gives no total for the list, or if its total says what it includes (shown prices, shipping read or a minimum) and names rows whose VAT is not stated or that are left out. FAIL if a total is called delivered while one of its rows has no shipping read ("excluding fees"), if it adds a price marked excluding VAT without saying so, or if it says shipping is unknown while it adds rows whose shipping was read.
Formatting does not matter: only substance decides.
