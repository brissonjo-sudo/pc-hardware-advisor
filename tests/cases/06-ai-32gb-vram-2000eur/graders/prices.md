PASS if every price shown carries: a date, the seller name, a seller type (direct retailer, marketplace, refurbisher, private party, manufacturer store), the country, and either a landed price (VAT, duties, shipping) or an explicit 'excluding fees' mention; OR if the answer gives no prices and says it could not verify them. FAIL if any price is presented without date and seller, or a marketplace seller is presented as a direct retailer.
Formatting does not matter: only substance decides.
