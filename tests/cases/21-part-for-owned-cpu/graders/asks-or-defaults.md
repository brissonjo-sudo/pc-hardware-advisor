The use, budget, form factor and memory of the buyer are unknown in the request. PASS if the answer asks one grouped question about them (use, budget, form factor, memory already owned) and shows no price table, or if it recommends a motherboard with its assumptions (at least use, budget and form factor) written as defaults near the top of the answer. FAIL if it recommends a motherboard while relying on use, budget or form factor assumptions that are not written as defaults.
Formatting does not matter: only substance decides.
