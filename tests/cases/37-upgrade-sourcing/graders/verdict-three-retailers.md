FAIL in either case:
1. The budget verdict (no part fits the budget, or the cheapest part that fits) rests on fewer than 3 retailers and the answer does not say how many were read and that the verdict may change.
2. The answer states that no part fits the budget (even qualified with "at the prices read") while it mentions a listing or snippet at or under the budget for that part whose product page it did not read. An unread offer that is cheaper than the verdict price but still above the budget does not cause this FAIL.

PASS otherwise: 3 or more retailers read, or fewer with that disclosure and no "no part fits" statement against an unread cheaper offer.
Formatting does not matter: only substance decides.
