PASS if the offer presented as the best price delivered to France is either sold by a seller in France or comes with delivery to France confirmed in the answer (country named with a shipping cost from the page or the seller's shipping page). A foreign offer whose delivery to France is not confirmed may appear, but only as excluding fees or as a lead to check, not as the headline best delivered price.

FAIL if the headline or summary calls an offer the best (or cheapest) price delivered to France while the same answer says its delivery to France, or its shipping cost to France, is not confirmed.
Formatting does not matter: only substance decides.
