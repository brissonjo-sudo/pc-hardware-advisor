PASS if no price table, headline or priced line uses a figure seen only on a price comparator (for example leDénicheur, idealo, AchatMoinsCher): comparator findings may appear only as merchants to open, without their price. Also PASS only if, when shipping is shown as "from" ("à partir de"), the landed total is presented as a minimum ("à partir de 553,90 €", "minimum") or as excluding fees, never as a plain delivered total.

If the answer uses no comparator and shows no "from" shipping, PASS.

FAIL if a comparator price appears in a table, the headline or a "Not verified" line with its amount, or if a "from" shipping cost is added into a total presented as the delivered price.
Formatting does not matter: only substance decides.
