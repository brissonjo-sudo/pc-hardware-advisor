## Section 301 date conflict (resolution)

1. Same measure, two wordings. Both lines point at the 178 China technology-transfer exclusions (HTS 9903.88.69 and 9903.88.70) from the 2018 Section 301 action. USTR press release of 2025-11-26 says "extended until November 10, 2026". The Federal Register notice (90 FR 55232, 2025-12-01) says "through 11:59 p.m. eastern daylight time on November 9, 2026". The Federal Register text is the operative one: 2026-11-09 is right, "10 Nov 2026" is the press-release rounding (the exclusion stops applying from 00:00 on 10 Nov).
2. Both are now stale. A USTR notice dated 2026-11-06 (listed on the USTR exclusions page, PDF fetched today) "further extend[s] the exclusions through 11:59 p.m. eastern time on January 9, 2027" for all 178 exclusions, effective 2026-11-10. So "No extension found" and "Decision expected within days" in international-buying.md:13 are wrong as of today. The Federal Register citation of the 2026-11-06 notice was not obtained.
3. Scope not resolved. The notices define the exclusions by U.S. notes 20(vvv)(i)-(iv) and 20(www) and do not list 8473.30 or 8471 lines in the text read, so whether GPUs and motherboards are inside is still unconfirmed.

### Worked-example recomputation

Example 1 with the file's own rates, no change: 340 x 0.90 = 306.00; 20% x 306.00 = 61.20; max(14.60, 2.55% x 61.20 = 1.56) = 14.60; 2% x 306.00 = 6.12; 306.00 + 61.20 + 14.60 + 6.12 = 387.92. Arithmetic correct (rule: all four lines). With the confirmed UHF: VAT (306 + 2) x 20% = 61.60; landed 306 + 2 + 61.60 + 14.60 + 6.12 = 390.32. With the DHL Germany fee as proxy (2% x 61.60 = 1.23, minimum 12.50): 388.22. Example 2: 60 x 0.75 = 45.00; (45 + 8) x 20% = 10.60; 2% x 63.60 = 1.272, rounded 1.27; 53.00 + 10.60 + 1.27 = 64.87. Arithmetic correct. No arithmetic error in either example; the Example 1 error is a missing input (UHF), not a calculation slip.
