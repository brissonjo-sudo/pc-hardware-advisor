# Retailer trust criteria

Last checked: 2026-11-07. Values change: verify online before concluding.

Use this file to judge a seller before recommending it. Check each criterion on the page you read. Legal anchors are EU-centred; for other countries state that the anchor was not researched. Confidence is the research's own.

## 1. Trust criteria (research dated 2026-11-07)

| Criterion | How to check on a page | Why | Legal anchor | Src | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seller clearly identified | Legal name, postal address, phone or email in page or imprint. On a marketplace: "sold by" line and trader / non-trader statement | Buyer must know who holds warranty and withdrawal rights | Dir. 2011/83/EU Art. 6(1)(b)-(c); marketplace trader flag Art. 6a(1)(b) (inserted by Dir. 2019/2161) | [1][2] | high (Art. 6); medium (trader flag) |
| Total price incl. VAT before checkout | Label "incl. VAT"; total visible before payment step | Hidden fees are a common complaint | Dir. 2011/83/EU Art. 6(1)(e) | [2] | high |
| Shipping cost and delivery time before checkout | Fee and date/range on product or cart page | Checkout surprises | Art. 6(1)(e): delivery charges disclosed or stated as payable | [2] | high (costs); low (delivery date rule not verified) |
| Real stock shown | Per-SKU status ("in stock", "ships in X days"); no "available soon" without date | Preorders and phantom stock | None verified | n/a | low |
| Price-reduction reference | Strike-through or "-X %" must rest on the lowest price of the previous 30 days; look for price history or dated change | Stops inflated list prices | Dir. 98/6/EC Art. 6a(1)-(2); CJEU C-330/23 (Aldi Sud) applies it to percentage wording | [1][4][5] | high (Art. 6a); medium (ruling) |
| Price update date | Date of last price change or history graph | Needed to apply the 30-day rule | No explicit date duty found; best practice | [1] | medium |
| Withdrawal right 14 days | Returns page states 14 days from receipt; return address and carrier reachable from buyer's country | EU consumer right; exceptions exist (Art. 16) | Dir. 2011/83/EU Art. 9(1)-(2) | [2] | high |
| Legal guarantee 2 years | Warranty page states 2-year legal guarantee for EU buyers; seller ships to buyer's country | Applies regardless of seller policy | Dir. (EU) 2019/771 Art. 10(1) | [3] | high (Art. 10(1)); Art. 11 is "Burden of proof" (one-year presumption, two years at Member State option, Art. 11(2)); Art. 13 is "Remedies for lack of conformity" (eur-lex.europa.eu, read 2026-11-08) |
| Secure payment, no mandatory bank transfer | Card, PayPal or platform payment offered; no "bank transfer only", no payment outside the site | Bank-transfer-only is a fake-shop signal | Not verified in law; consumer checklists | [6][7] | medium |
| Verifiable history | Company register entry, VAT number, company age; reviews on more than one source | Fake shops are often days old | DSA Art. 30 requires marketplaces to trace traders (secondary) | [8][9] | medium |
| No fake or purchased reviews | Review page explains verification (purchase proof, dates) | Reviews can be bought | Dir. 2005/29/EC Art. 7(6) and Annex I; UK DMCC Act 2024 ban from 6 Apr 2025; US FTC 16 CFR Part 465 (all via search) | [10][11][12] | medium |
| Page readable without circumvention | Terms and price load without a captcha bypass, login wall or workaround | Project rule: never recommend bypassing bot protection | Project rule, not law | n/a | high (as rule) |

How to apply: score each row as pass / fail / not shown. Report "not shown" honestly. Two or more fails on identity, payment or returns means do not recommend.

## 3. Hardware scam patterns (dated 2026-11-07)

| Pattern | Red flags | Src | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relabelled older GPUs | Name in GPU-Z differs from die or device ID; newer GPU-Z shows a [FAKE] prefix. Check the GPU-Z version first, older versions may flag genuine cards | [17][18] | medium |
| Used GPU with swapped or harvested VRAM, tampered stickers | Memory type or size in GPU-Z differs from spec database; screws or sticker missing or mismatched | [18] | medium |
| Fake-capacity SSDs and flash drives | No source found in research | gap | low |
| Used GPU sold with an unrelated GPU-Z screenshot | Screenshot not matching serial, date or seller; ask for a live check. Not verified in research | gap | low |
| Mining cards sold as new or lightly used | No warranty, unusual wear, seller refuses inspection. Not verified in research | gap | low |
| "Too cheap" marketplace listings | Price far below market; new account; stock photo; no returns policy | [6][7] | medium |
| Payment outside the platform | Request for bank transfer, WhatsApp or direct payment | [6][7] | medium |
| Drop-shipping storefronts with imitation brand names | Brand spelling slightly altered; imprint names a different company; very recent domain | [19] (user reports) | low |
| Review manipulation | Many reviews in a short window; generic praise; non-buyers | [10][11][12] | medium |

## 4. Sites and seller types to avoid (sourced only)

- No individual site is named. Research found no consumer-authority or major-outlet page naming a PC component seller. Trustpilot and Fakeshop-Finder entries are user reports or third-party checks: do not treat them as established. Do not name a site as a scam without a cited official source.
- Seller types to avoid, with basis:
  - No imprint or company identity: checklists and Watchlist claims (not opened). Medium.
  - Bank-transfer-only or off-platform payment: checklists. Medium.
  - Strike-through prices without a 30-day lowest-price basis: Art. 6a, Dir. 98/6/EC. High for the rule, medium for enforcement.
  - Mass fake or purchased reviews: UK DMCC and US FTC rules (secondary). Medium.

## 5. Reputation sources and limits (2026-11-07)

| Source | Limit | Src | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|

| Google reviews | Which? reported Google and Facebook did not filter fake reviews well; article undated | [21] | low |
| Trusted Shops | Buyer Protection covers non-delivery and refund failure; warranty cases excluded. Sources conflict on covered order value (EUR 100 vs EUR 2,500). Shop must answer 95% of inquiries within 5 days | [22] | medium |
| Klicksafe checklist | Consumer checklist for fake shops; no date verified | [6] | medium |
| Reddit | Not researched | n/a | low |

Rules: never rely on one reputation source; cross-check at least two; treat star averages as weak evidence; verify identity (imprint, register entry) first.
